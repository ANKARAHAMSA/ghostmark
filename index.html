How it works

01
Upload
Any raster image — PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP. Resolution doesn't matter.
02
Encode
Your text is written into the 2 least significant bits of each R, G, B channel. Max shift: ±3 per value.
03
Export PNG
Download as lossless PNG. The watermark survives indefinitely — JPEG recompression would destroy it.
04
Verify
Drop any watermarked PNG into the Validator. Your payload is extracted and displayed instantly.
